# Rice / Paddy / धान

General decision guide, not a local crop calendar or pesticide label. Transplanting or direct-seeding dates depend on rainfall, variety and district; confirm with the local KVK. Check water level and field drainage rather than assuming that standing water is always needed. Inspect leaves and stems for damage and confirm whether the cause is pest, nutrient or weather before applying anything. Prefer clean planting material, field sanitation and locally advised IPM. No chemical dose or yield claim is supplied here.
