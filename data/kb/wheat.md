# Wheat / गेहूँ

General decision guide, not a local crop calendar or pesticide label. Sowing dates vary by district, season, variety and irrigation; ask the local KVK for this season's window. Before irrigation, check soil moisture near the root zone and the latest local forecast. Walk the field regularly; if yellowing, spots or insects appear, photograph the affected plant and get the cause identified before treatment. Remove badly affected plant material where practical, keep field hygiene, and use local KVK advice for IPM. No chemical dose or yield claim is supplied here.
