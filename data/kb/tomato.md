# Tomato / टमाटर

General decision guide, not a local crop calendar or pesticide label. Nursery and transplanting windows vary by district and season; ask the local horticulture office or KVK. Water consistently but avoid prolonged leaf wetness and waterlogging. For leaf spots, wilting or fruit damage, separate affected plants where feasible and identify the cause before treatment. Use clean seedlings, sanitation and locally advised IPM first. No chemical dose or yield claim is supplied here.
