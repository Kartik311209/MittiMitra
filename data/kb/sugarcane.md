# Sugarcane / गन्ना

General decision guide, not a local crop calendar or pesticide label. Planting window, seed-sett choice and spacing vary by agro-climatic zone; check with the local KVK or sugar mill extension office. Watch for water stress and waterlogging, and inspect for shoot or stem damage. Use healthy planting material and field sanitation; get pest or disease identified before control. No chemical dose or yield claim is supplied here.
