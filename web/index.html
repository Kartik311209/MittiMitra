THE PLATFORM

One place for the next useful step.

From understanding a soil photo to finding an official resource nearby, each part of MittiMitra is designed around a farmer’s actual decision.

01 / SOIL◎

Soil-photo exploration

Prototype NPK and moisture estimates from a photo, shown with a clear warning that only a laboratory test can confirm nutrient values.

Prototype available locally
02 / LOCAL⌖

Location-aware resources

State and district context connects farmers to official schemes and nearby soil-testing laboratory information.

In the current portal
03 / SUPPORT✳

Kisan Mitra assistant

A conversational interface for general farming questions, with expert referral for animal-health and other high-stakes concerns.

Experimental
04 / RESEARCH✺

Crop-health research

Indian field-photo datasets are being evaluated for crop-disease signals. This feature remains off until the model is reliable enough to test with farmers.

Not live yet
